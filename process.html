The Execution Framework

A disciplined 6-stage journey from concept to key handover.

We eliminate ambiguity with milestone rigor, fixed-price transparency, physical material samples, and proactive engineering.

1
Stage 01 • Weeks 1 – 2

Discovery & Spatial Audit

We conduct an immersive spatial audit of your property, analyzing structural constraints, orientation towards natural daylight, acoustic challenges, and your lifestyle aspirations.

Milestone Deliverable: Architectural Brief Document, Laser As-Built 3D Point-Cloud Survey & Project Feasibility Report.
2
Stage 02 • Weeks 3 – 5

Concept Development & Moodboards

Our principal architects translate your brief into an emotional visual narrative. We establish tactile material harmonies, color spectrums, ceiling hierarchies, and architectural focal points.

Milestone Deliverable: Curated Physical Material Sample Board Box, Conceptual 3D Vignettes & Mood Narrative Deck.
3
Stage 03 • Weeks 6 – 9

Space Planning & Schematics

Every square millimeter is optimized. We generate precision 2D CAD architectural layouts, reflected ceiling plans (RCP), lighting scene schematics, and electrical/MEP routing.

Milestone Deliverable: Complete Architectural Blueprint Set, Photorealistic 8K 3D Renders & Virtual Reality Walkthrough.
4
Stage 04 • Weeks 10 – 12

Material Selection & Bespoke Millwork

We coordinate directly with master quarry stone masters in Italy and joinery workshops. Detailed shop drawings for modular kitchens, vanity units, and custom furniture are locked in.

Milestone Deliverable: Comprehensive Bill of Quantities (BOQ), Fixed-Price Turnkey Contract & Quarry Slab Tagging.
5
Stage 05 • Construction Phase

Turnkey Execution & Site Management

Our dedicated project architects oversee site construction with zero tolerance for error. Demolition, MEP rough-in, false ceiling framing, stone installation, and custom cabinetry fitting are synchronized.

Milestone Deliverable: Bi-Weekly Drone & 360° Photo Progress Reports, Quality Assurance Checkpoints & Snagging Audits.
6
Stage 06 • Final Milestone

Handover & White-Glove Styling

Deep professional cleaning, fine art installation, bespoke drapery steaming, scent diffusion, and Lutron lighting scene calibration culminate in an unforgettable welcome home.

Milestone Deliverable: Bespoke Atelier Home Care Box, Warranty Portfolio, Appliance Care Manuals & Master Key Handover.
