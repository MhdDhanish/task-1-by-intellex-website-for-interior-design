Studio Identity & Ethos

Sculpting spaces with quiet luxury, emotional depth, and architectural rigor.

Founded in 2016, Fenyx Interiors unites master architects, Italian joinery artisans, and turnkey engineers to create timeless environments for private residences and forward-thinking enterprises.

Our Origins

Born from a pursuit of timeless materiality.

Fenyx Interiors began with a singular conviction: that true luxury is not defined by excess, but by the restraint of form, the authenticity of raw materials, and the silent choreography of natural light.

Over a decade of practice across London, New York, Geneva, and Dubai, our atelier has grown into an international collective of spatial visionaries. We refuse generic templates and short-lived trends, prioritizing hand-selected Italian travertine, patinated brass millwork, and acoustic harmony that endures across generations.

Foundational Principles

The Four Pillars of Fenyx

Every line we draw and every space we build is anchored in four non-negotiable standards.

01

Material Authenticity

We source raw travertine, European white oak, solid brushed bronze, and unbleached Belgian linen. No synthetic imitations, ever.

02

Spatial Choreography

Every floor plan is engineered for seamless ergonomics, sensory transition, acoustic quietude, and maximized natural daylight.

03

Artisan Precision

From concealed pocket doors to bookmatched marble veins, our tolerance standards are measured in sub-millimeter increments.

04

Turnkey Integrity

Single-point accountability. We manage procurement, contractors, customs, and final white-glove styling on time and on budget.
